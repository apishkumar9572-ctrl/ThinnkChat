💬 Messaging
🗑️
How do I delete a message?
Long press on any message (or right-click on desktop), then select the "Delete" option. You can only delete messages you sent yourself. Once deleted, a message cannot be recovered.
✏️
Can I edit a message after sending it?
Yes! Long press the message and select "Edit". You can only edit within 15 minutes of sending. Edited messages show an "Edited" label.
📎
What's the size limit for sending files/photos?
Images: max 10 MB per image
Documents (PDF, DOCX etc.): max 25 MB
Videos: max 50 MB

The free plan includes 1 GB of total storage. Premium gets unlimited storage.
📡
My message isn't delivering — what do I do?
First, check your internet connection. If it's fine, refresh the page. If the other person is offline, the message stays queued and delivers once they come back online. A double tick ✓✓ means it has been delivered.
👤 Account & Security
🔐
How do I change my password?
Go to Settings → Account → Security. Click "Change Password". Enter your old password, then set a new one. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.
❌
I want to permanently delete my account
You'll find the "Delete Account" option under Settings → Account → Danger Zone. After deletion, you have 30 days to recover your account, after which it's permanently gone. All data, messages, and files will be deleted.
🛡️
How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
Go to Settings → Security → Two-Factor Auth. You can set up 2FA using Google Authenticator or SMS. Highly recommended for your account's security.
💳 Billing & Plans
💰
What's the refund policy? Can I get my money back?
Yes! There's a 7-day money-back guarantee. Request a refund within 7 days of purchase for a full refund — no questions asked. Refunds aren't possible after 7 days. Processing takes 5–7 business days.
⭐
How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Go to Settings → Billing → Manage Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply from the next billing cycle. Moving back to the free plan disables premium features, but your data stays safe.
🧾
Where can I find my invoice/receipt?
All invoices are available under Settings → Billing → Payment History. You can download them as PDFs. An invoice is also automatically emailed to you after every payment.
🔒 Privacy & Data
🔒
Are my messages secure? How does encryption work?
Direct chats on ThinnkChat are end-to-end encrypted. That means only you and the other person can read the messages — not even ThinnkChat can. Group chats use transport-level encryption.
📦
How do I export my data/chat history?
Submit a request under Settings → Privacy → Export My Data. Within 24 hours, you'll receive a ZIP file by email containing all your messages, media, and account info.
